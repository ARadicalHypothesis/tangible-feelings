# The Tangible Feelings wiki (2011–2012)

The workshops had their own wiki at `tangible-feelings.wikispaces.com`, used for course information and resources. Wikispaces closed in 2018. The pages below come from copies saved by the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine. The wiki's "final projects", "lectures" and "interesting links" pages and its uploaded files were not saved.

## Home page

> Tangible Feelings Workshops is a series of international events devoted to fostering a discussion around BCI and EEG technologies (new generation brain-computer interfaces, like IBVA, Emotiv, Neurosky) and their aesthetic implications in the field of contemporary digital arts. The workshops, taught by artists and researchers Mattia Casalegno and Enzo Varriale, offer both skill-based learning and theoretical discussion about the impact of BCI technology in the worlds of art and science.
>
> The first workshop was held at iMal in Brussels as part of the Tangible Feelings festival, a 3-days event comprising also a symposium and an exhibition that brought together artists, scientists, researchers and experts in EEG and BCI technologies.
>
> The second appointment was held in Taipei in January 2012. Organized by the Transjourney Future Media festival and hosted by the Digital Art Center Taipei, this 3-days workshop was focused on creating audiovisual and interactive applications with the Neurosky technology and the max/msp/jitter, Processing and Arduino programming environments.

## Tangible Feelings Taipei

> Tangible Feelings Taipei: January 2nd, 3rd, 4th 2012, 10am to 6pm, Digital Art Center, No. 180, Fuhua Rd., Shihlin Dist., Taipei City.
>
> A hands-on workshop about neuro-technology and art, with a focus on the Emotiv software and hardware.
>
> This 3-days workshop led by Mattia Casalegno (co-author of *Unstable Empathy*) will kick off with an introduction on the EEG signal, and how it can be manipulated to perform different kinds of data visualizations and sonifications. The Emotiv SDK will be successively introduced along with basic techniques to use Emotiv data with the Max/MSP/Jitter and Processing environments.
>
> This workshop is targeted to media artists, designers, and creative developers with good or medium knowledge of Max/MSP/Jitter or Processing. Emotiv EPOC headsets and Emotiv SDK software will be provided, but participants need to provide their own Windows laptops (Emotiv is currently supported only on Windows) with their software of choice installed.

The Taipei workshop used both Emotiv EPOC and NeuroSky headsets. The original page also listed the seven participants with their bios and contact details. That list is left out here for their privacy.
